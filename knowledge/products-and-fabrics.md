## Our fabrics

Our adire is tied and dyed in indigo by hand, so the pattern shifts a little from piece to piece. Our linen is washed before it is cut, so it is soft from the first wear.

## Caring for your pieces

These tips apply to our adire and other hand dyed pieces.

Wash cold, inside out. This keeps indigo deep and stripes crisp.

Dry in the shade. Strong sun fades hand dyed cloth.

Iron while slightly damp. Linen relaxes faster and creases less.
